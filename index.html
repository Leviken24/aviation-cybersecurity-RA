Core Research Findings Summary (Elmarady & Rahouma, 2021)

IEEE Access Paper Analysis
Communication Link
Highest Risk: VHF Voice
Lowest Risk: CPDLC

VHF voice lacks encryption; spoofing triggers intolerable hazards. CPDLC data link has selective addressing.

Radio Navigation Aids
Highest Risk: GNSS (GPS)
Lowest Risk: ILS / VOR

Satellite signals are extremely weak (-160 dBW) and easily jammed by cheap PPDs. Ground aids require specialized local transmitters.

Aeronautical Surveillance
Highest Risk: ADS-B
Lowest Risk: SSR / PSR

ADS-B broadcasts open 1090 MHz squitters prone to ghost targets. Secondary Radar (SSR) uses directional interrogation.

Networking & Architecture
SWIM Risk: Medium (Tolerable)
Key Threat: MITM / IP Attacks

Net-centric IP exchanges introduce standard cyber vectors. Private IP networks & manual backups prevent direct crashes.

Primary Scientific Contributions of the Authors

As highlighted in Section I (Page 3-4), previous surveys either addressed only isolated subsystems (e.g. only ADS-B in [16], only CPDLC/GPS in [21], only RF interference in [10]) or conducted generic perceptual opinion polls [19] without an algorithmic quantitative risk formula.

The proposed methodology fills this gap by delivering the first continuous, quantitative (r = L × I) and qualitative (R ∈ {Acceptable, Tolerable, Intolerable}) cybersecurity risk assessment framework encompassing all CNS/ATM technologies and net-centric SWIM.
